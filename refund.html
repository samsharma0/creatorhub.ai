Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [18/8/2026]

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for paid plans on CreatorHub AI.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation will stop future billing, but you will continue to have access to paid features until the end of your current billing period.

2. Refund Eligibility

  • Refund requests must be made within [7] days of the original purchase date.
  • Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis, particularly in cases of duplicate billing, unauthorized charges, or technical issues that prevented you from using the service.
  • We do not offer refunds for partial use of a billing period, or for dissatisfaction with AI-generated results, since these are provided as suggestions and outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at [satyamkotri@gmail.com] with your account email and payment reference/transaction ID. We aim to respond within [3-5] business days.

4. Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within [5-10] business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. Free Trial / Free Tier

If a free trial or free tier is offered, no charges apply until you actively upgrade to a paid plan. No refund is applicable for the free tier since no payment is made.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact Us

For any billing or refund questions, reach out to: [satyamkotri@gmail.com]
